On 27 March 1977, two Boeing 747s collided on a foggy runway at Los Rodeos airport, Tenerife. 583 people died. It is still the deadliest accident in aviation history, and the last link in the chain was a few unclear words on the radio. This is how it happened, and how it changed the way every pilot speaks.
