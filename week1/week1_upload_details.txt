TITLE:
Why Is Smoking Banned on Planes? ✈️ #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
On 11 July 1973, a fire in a rear lavatory of Varig Flight 820, most likely started by a cigarette in the waste bin, filled the cabin with smoke near Paris. 123 people died and 11 survived. The FAA banned smoking in aircraft lavatories that year; US airlines banned it on all flights by 2000. Ashtrays are still required by FAA rules.

TITLE:
Why Do Pilots Repeat Every Instruction? ✈️ #shorts

DESCRIPTION:
On 27 March 1977, two Boeing 747s collided on a fog-covered runway at Los Rodeos Airport, Tenerife. 583 people died and 61 survived. A blocked radio call and unclear wording were part of the chain, so phraseology was standardized worldwide: 'takeoff' is said only with a real clearance, and key instructions are read back.
