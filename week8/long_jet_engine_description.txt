How does a jet engine turn air into more than 100,000 pounds of thrust? Step by step: Newton's third law, Frank Whittle's first engine run in 1937, then suck, squeeze, bang, blow — the fan, the compressor, the combustor and the turbine. Plus how turbine blades survive gas hotter than their melting point, why bypass air makes about 80% of a turbofan's takeoff thrust, and the 134,300 lb world-record engine test.
