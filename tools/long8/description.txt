For 27 years, Concorde carried passengers at twice the speed of sound. So why did the most famous airliner ever built fail? Exploding costs, vanishing orders, the 1973 oil crisis, the sonic boom, fuel and maintenance bills, the July 2000 crash and the travel slump after 9/11 all played a part.
