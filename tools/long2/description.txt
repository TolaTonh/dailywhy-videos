Almost every rule in aviation was written after something went terribly wrong. Here are 7 plane accidents that changed how we fly, and the safety rule each one left behind: radar control, no smoking, clear radio words, floor lighting, wind shear warnings, aging-aircraft inspections, and pilot experience and rest rules.
