On 23 July 1983, a brand-new Boeing 767 ran out of fuel at 41,000 feet over Canada. Both engines stopped and the cockpit screens went dark. This is how a unit mix-up left it with less than half the fuel it needed, and how a glider-pilot captain landed it on an old air base being used as a racetrack. All 69 people on board survived.
